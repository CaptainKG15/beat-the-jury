    How it works: you pick a guess and a confidence, and only a hash of it goes on-chain. After everyone has committed, you reveal. Three juror readings of the page are taken by each validator and the majority is the verdict; validators must agree on the verdict, so wording never matters. Points follow a proper scoring rule, so being honest about your confidence pays best (0 to 340 per round).

    Limits: the jury is LLM readings of one page at one moment, so borderline claims can fail to settle and be retried. If you clear this browser's data before revealing, your secret is lost. On Studio the app uses a throwaway key kept in this browser, which holds no funds.
