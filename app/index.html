How scoring works. Your guess is hidden until the reveal. You choose a confidence from 40% to 90%, and a proper scoring rule pays out up to 340 points per round. Being overconfident and wrong costs the most, so honest confidence scores best.

Limits. The jury is LLM readings of one page at one moment, so borderline claims can fail to settle and need another try. If you clear this browser's data before revealing, your secret is lost and you score 0. On Studio this page uses a throwaway key kept in your browser that holds no funds.
